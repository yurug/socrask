Narrative rail

One dominant sequence, with the meaningful transition made explicit.

Perception

The response crosses a five-step path; the sentinel removes the waiting gap.

An answer becomes agent contextReading order → left to right
00:00Human answers

The browser records a question, decision, or mastery signal.

+20 msServer appends

The event receives a cursor and enters the session stream.

+35 msSentinel wakes

A matching human event ends the follow immediately.

+80 msAgent reads

The exact user response returns with its actor and cursor.

Next turnCursor advances

Replay stays safe; already-seen events are not forgotten or duplicated.

Quality bar

A visual is successful before it is beautiful.

Beauty here is coherent hierarchy, balanced density, stable semantics, and an immediate reading path. The composition should make the intended relationship hard to miss and every other relationship quiet.
